What the margins mean

Best-class: how far the winning cluster leads the best cluster outside its tract, in logits, read from the six classes stored per streamline. It reads at most 8 (the clip), and can read high where the stored classes were all the tract's own.

Mass: log-odds of the tract as a whole, with the probability of the classes not stored counted against the tract, so it is a lower bound.

Low margins mark streamlines whose tract changed across repeated TractCloud runs: below 0.25 logits, 79% changed; above 8, 0.3%.

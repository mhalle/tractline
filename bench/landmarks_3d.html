cIIIs04 tract landmarks

A healthy adult (MASiVar cohort III, subject 04). The tractography pipeline ran 17 times on one scan: once as acquired and 16 times on copies with its noise drawn again. The same subject was scanned three more times, once in the same session and once in each of two later sessions. Shells show how much of a tract the 17 runs agree on. Dots show where a real rescan put the tract outside the ensemble's 80% envelope. Fans show single streamlines at landmark sites, drawn from all 17 runs and from the rescans.

Research prototype. This shows how reproducible the pipeline's output is, not where the fibers truly run. The sites are found from the tracts themselves (no T1 or atlas), so their anatomical names are approximate.

Reading the view

Core: voxels where the tract is present (2 or more of its streamlines) in at least 9 of the 17 runs.

80% envelope: its level was fitted on two other subjects to hold 80% of a rescan's tract. For the tracts shown it holds of each rescan's tract voxels. Widest: any run.

Runner-up: rank the named tracts in each voxel by their mean streamline count over the 17 runs (leaving out Other, which leads in most voxels). The shell is where this tract comes second among them.

Dots: voxels where the chosen rescan has the tract but the 80% envelope does not: 8–16% of each rescan's tract voxels here. Clusters may mean the envelope is too tight, or a registration error, or a real difference between scans.

Fans: each seed near the site drawn once per run, colored by the tract that run gave it (or by that run's second choice), with the rescans' versions in white. Each site's fan colors are listed with the site.

Limits

One healthy subject, one scanner (Philips, 2.1 × 2.1 × 2.2 mm, b = 1000 and 2000). The rescans were registered rigidly and tracked on the reference grid. Shells are smoothed isosurfaces of vote counts; their thresholds set the apparent boundary. Coordinates are scanner millimeters in the patient's frame (R/L, A/P, S/I labels).

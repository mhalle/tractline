PAT16 tract envelopes

White-matter tracts near a temporal tumor, from 17 runs of the tractography pipeline: one on the scan as acquired, and 16 on copies of it with the scan's noise drawn again. Where the runs agree, a tract is drawn solidly; where they disagree, it spreads into a wider outline.

Research prototype. The outlines show how reproducible the pipeline's output is, not where the fibers truly run. Nothing here was checked against stimulation mapping or histology. Not for surgical planning.
